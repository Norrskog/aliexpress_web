    How to buy goods

    1. Find desired goods

      Use one of the following ways to search for goods:

      • Enter keywords into the search bar.

      • Go to Categories, and select one of them. Then select a subcategory on the top of the page to dive to a deeper level.

    2. Filter search results

      Filter and sort the goods list to get more specific search results. To do this, use filters in the sidebar.

    3. Add products to the cart

      3.1. Open a product page.

      3.2. Select all the required product parameters like color, size, and country of shipment.

      3.3. Click Add to cart.

    4. Create an order

      4.1. Go to Cart either from the product page or from the menu.

      4.2. Select the items you would like to order. Specify quantity.

      4.3. In the Total price section, click Buy. The checkout page will open.

    5. Checkout

      5.1. On the checkout page, provide the information:

      • In the Shipping Information section, specify a delivery address. In some cases, you can choose between the shipping and the picking up options. If the selection is available:

        • Select Shipping to get an order to your door or to pick it up from your post office.

        • Select Self-pickup to take the order from a dedicated pickup point.

      • In the Payment Methods section, specify a payment method. Here you can link several cards to your account and then select which one to pay with.

      • In the Order Review section, you can change the quantity of the items in the order. Use the plus and minus buttons, or insert the number manually.

      • In the Order Summary section, apply discount options:

        • Expand the coupon dropdowns and check if you have coupons provided to you by a seller or AliExpress, and select them if you have any.

        • If you have a promo code, fill it in the empty field and apply.

      5.2. Double-check all the fields and click Place Order. You will be redirected to your bank or your payment system page.

      5.3. Confirm the payment.

    Your order is placed. In the top menu, go to Orders, and click your order to open it. On its page, you can:

    • Check the status of the order.

    • Confirm receipt, when you receive the order.

    • Track the order. Here you can copy a tracking number that you can later use on a delivery service website to track where your order is.

    • Get help with your order from the support team or from a seller.
